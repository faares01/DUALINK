Not another cloud drive.

It is the small, thoughtful bridge between the computers you already use.

01

Link only what matters

Choose any pair of folders. Downloads can meet Downloads; Projects can meet D:\Projects.

02

Send across with intent

Choose a file, choose its destination, then let DUALINK deliver it when the other system is ready.

03

Keep the safe version

Conflicts ask before they act. Deleted files wait in Safe Delete. Earlier versions remain restorable.

BUILT WITH RESTRAINT

Your folders are yours.

DUALINK accesses only folders you explicitly approve in the desktop app. Your account uses Google sign-in; database credentials and file storage keys stay on the server, never in the browser.
